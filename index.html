Scope

What I review

Source review

The parts of your code that can lose or leak funds, named up front:

  • entropy, seed and key generation
  • derivation and descriptors (BIP-32, 39, 86, 380s)
  • PSBT building and signing, change and address checks
  • backups, seed storage, memory and wipe paths
  • Lightning payments, swaps and channel funds
  • pool shares and payouts

Release and signing

How a user knows the build they run is the build you made:

  • reproducible builds and checksums
  • release signing and key handling
  • CI workflows, tokens and permissions
  • dependency updates

After you ship fixes, one fix check is part of the quote: the changed code is read again against each finding, and you hear which ones are closed.

Process

How it works

  1. You send the repository, the commit, the parts that matter to you and your deadline.
  2. Within two business days I reply with the scope, what is left out, and a fixed price.
  3. The review runs. The report names the commit, links every finding to a file and line, and gives its impact, how likely it is, a suggested fix and what was not read. If you want, it comes with patches and regression tests.
  4. You fix. The fixes get checked once, and you hear which findings are closed.

Findings stay private until you have shipped a fix or until a date we agree on before I start. After that I may publish them, and you may publish the report.

Pricing

What sets the price

  • What the code does. Key generation and signing get read line by line. Screens and settings get a lighter pass. Two thousand lines of signing code can take longer than twenty thousand lines of interface.
  • How much of it is in scope, counting the code that matters, not tests or vendored libraries.
  • Languages and layers. Firmware in C or Rust, a Go backend and a web frontend are three reviews in one.
  • What it follows. Code that implements a BIP, or has a design document, is faster to check.
  • Your deadline. A rush costs more.
  • Extras, such as patches with tests or a summary written for your users.

A report you allow me to publish after the fix costs a little less. Most reviews take one to three weeks. Quotes are in US dollars, paid in bitcoin, on-chain or over Lightning.

Method

How I check

The review is done by AI models. Several read the code on their own and raise claims. Other models then try to knock each claim down, and most do not survive. What is left is traced through the code by a frontier model and checked against the BIP or the project's test vectors, and anything rated medium or above is read again by a model released only to vetted security researchers. Every report goes out under my name, and I answer for it.

The review reads source. Nothing is built or run, and the report says so. A review that lists what it did not read is worth more than one that claims to have read everything.

Limits

What I won't do

  • Write a pass letter, a certificate, or a sentence saying your users' funds are safe. You get findings and a list of what was out of scope.
  • Take a seed, a private key or a live macaroon from you, for any reason.
  • Charge for a report I sent you unasked. Those are free and never come with a payment request; paid work starts only when you ask for it.
